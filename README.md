# 🎄 Christmas Gift Tracker & Holiday Budget Planner App (AY Studios Edition)

A high-end, responsive Web Application designed to track Christmas gifts, recipients, budgets, idea bank brainstorming, and store shopping lists — crafted to match the exact aesthetic of AY Studios' top-selling Etsy holiday planning app.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **🎅 Festive Garland Hero & Running Countdown Clock**
   - High-definition pine garland with shiny red & gold baubles, red velvet ribbons, golden bells, and Santa Claus.
   - Live second-by-second countdown clock (Days, Hours, Minutes, Seconds) ticking towards Christmas.

2. **💰 100% Dynamic & Editable Holiday Budget**
   - Set your total holiday budget target manually, or calculate automatically with one click (`Sum from Recipients` or `Sum from Gifts`).
   - Mathematically derived metrics: Total Spent, Left to Spend, Donut progress percentages, and Per-Recipient progress bars (Zero hardcoded fake figures).
   - "Start Blank (My Own Data)" to enter your own custom Christmas list from scratch, or "Load Demo Data" to explore sample entries.

3. **🎁 Dedicated Gifts Checklist Page (Bought, Wrapped, Sent)**
   - Fits 100% within the screen width without horizontal overflow.
   - Text size, padding, and fonts styled cleanly for optimal readability.
   - Interactive Search box and Filter dropdowns (by Group and Stage: To Buy, Bought, Wrapped, Sent).
   - Custom deep evergreen checkboxes (`#266e40`) with crisp white SVG checkmarks.
   - Instant live table summary strip showing item counts, total planned, total spent, and remaining balance.

4. **👥 Dedicated Recipients & Budgets Page**
   - High-level overview banner tracking Total People, Total Allocated, Total Spent, and Over-Budget counts.
   - Group filter pills (`All People`, `Family`, `Friends`, `Kids`, `Coworkers`).
   - One dedicated budget card per recipient with color-coded live spending bars and holiday icons.

5. **💡 Dedicated Idea Bank Page**
   - Brainstorm gift ideas before committing to purchases.
   - One-click `+ Convert to Gift` automatically transfers the idea directly into your active gifts table!

6. **🛍️ Dedicated Store Shopping List Page**
   - Unbought gifts automatically grouped by store (e.g. Amazon, Target, Macy's, Best Buy).
   - Check off items while out shopping to instantly update your progress.

7. **🌙 Cosy Dark Mode & ❄️ Interactive Snowfall**
   - One-tap switch between warm holiday parchment theme and cosy dark mode.
   - Smooth canvas snowflake snowfall with on/off toggle.

---

## 🚀 How to Open and Use

Simply open `index.html` in your browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Safari, Firefox).
No server or build installation required — everything runs natively and stores data in your browser's LocalStorage!
